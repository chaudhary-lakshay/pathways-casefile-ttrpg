### Finding 1 — the coverage instrument has a second defect, and it produces false LOWS

`6db` fixed the `MECHANICAL` filter. A different defect remains, in the join rather than the filter.

> **Correction, added after the fix landed.** This section originally read the six spans below as content files declaring the wrong chapter id, and prescribed correcting the declarations. **That was backwards, and acting on it would have made the report worse.** The partition was the defect, not the declarations: the book starts several chapters *mid-page*, so bucketing lines by page credited everything above a heading to the chapter that had not started yet. Measured against the headings, five of the six declarations were right all along — `common_modifiers.json` really does hold Appendix A material, printed on Appendix B's page — and "correcting" them would have moved Appendix A's quick-reference tables into the political appendix. The shipped fix partitions by heading line; see `tools/coverage.py`'s docstring. What follows is the corrected account, with the post-fix numbers.

`coverage.py` assigns each source line to a chapter by **page range**, and a page can hold the end of one chapter and the start of the next. Every line above a mid-page heading was therefore credited to the wrong chapter — **57 mechanical lines repo-wide**. A content file claiming those lines under the chapter they genuinely belong to had its claim land in a bucket holding none of them, while the neighbouring chapter was charged for lines nobody had promised to extract. ### Finding 2 — 46% of the book's countable lines sit in chapters that are 100% extracted and ~30% applied

Chapters 9–12 (Sequence 9/8/7/6 potion effects) hold **1119 of 2427 mechanical lines — 46.1% of the book** — and all four report 100.0%. That figure is real as extraction and misleading as implementation.

`pathways.json` holds 88 blocks. `PotionDigestion.digest` reads exactly two fields: `stats` and `skills`. The rest of each block — the `ability` and `reinforcedAbilities` prose — is **displayed verbatim** (`PathwayScreen.kt:549`, `BoonDialog.kt:82`) and parsed by nothing. The book prints **237 SPI costs** across those blocks and this engine charges none of them. The Faceless's "Identity Confusion (2 SPI)" is a cost no pool ever debits. (An earlier draft of this section said "78 of 88 blocks carry a mechanical value", counted per block; 237 is the per-cost figure a test now pins.)

Split accordingly:

- non-potion chapters: **1110/1308 = 84.9%** claimed, and those claims are largely *applied*
- potion chapters: **1110/1119 = 99.2%** claimed, and those claims are largely *printed*

That is the single largest gap in the repo, and it is invisible to every percentage recorded to date. §4 records why it is a genuine gap rather than a defensible refusal, and why it nonetheless belongs in the parity number rather than the mechanics number: the rules are extracted and correct, and what is missing is the step that charges them.

**One precision, because it narrows the claim.** A subset of pathway abilities *does* reach the dice — but through a different table. `divination_awareness.json` (claimed under ch 6.5) structures per-pathway detection abilities into `freeRoll` and `bonus` fields, and `Detection.kt:337` consumes them. So the correct statement is not "pathway abilities are never applied"; it is that **`pathways.json`'s own `ability` field is parsed by nothing**, and where an ability reaches a roll it is because a separate chapter re-printed it in a structured table. That is the same shape as `6db`'s finding — a rule can be implemented from a source the chapter's own coverage number cannot see — and it means the 30%-applied estimate for ch 9–12 is a floor, not a point estimate.

### Finding 3 — there is no reference surface, so bucket B is empty by construction

Navigation is three screens (`MainActivity.kt:131`: `SHEET`, `PATHWAY`, `BUILDER`) plus the premade gallery. Every route requires a selected character; **nothing in the app browses the book.** Under the handoff's own rubric, all reference-only material fails bucket B automatically — Appendix B (political world), Appendix C (churches), the glossary, and chapter 7.5's summoning procedure have no surface regardless of extraction state. This is the dominant term in the 58% parity figure.

## 4. False gaps and false completeness

### False gaps — coverage says missing, implementation exists

- **Chapter 6, all 7 lines.** `fright_effects.json` claims 2924–2994; the table crosses the page 43→44 boundary. Chapter is complete.
- **Appendix B, 9 of 12 lines.** `common_modifiers.json` claims 9725–9745 under `"A"`; those lines print on page 160, which is `"B"`.
- **Appendix A, most of 32 lines.** The skill-difficulty ladder is `SkillLadder`, the combat summary is `ActiveDefenses`/`Wounds`/`ParryBlock`, the wounding table is `Wounds.multiplierFor`. An appendix restating implemented chapters reads 8.6% forever.
- **Chapter 5, ~15 of 40 lines.** Combat Reflexes' +1 (`ActiveDefenses.kt:182`), Brawling/Boxing/Wrestling parries (`ParryBlock.kt:48-75`), HP thresholds and unconsciousness (`Pools.kt`).
- **Chapter 7, ~6 of 25 lines.** HP Sacrifice (`AntiDivination.kt:137`), assistant bonuses (`RitualMode.supportBonus`).
- **Chapter 3, ~30 of 56 lines.** The attribute cost ladder is arithmetic in `AttributeCosts.costOf`, so it reads 0% by construction — the "coverage proxy is blind to multipliers" class.

**A table implemented as arithmetic, and a table restated in an appendix, both read as gaps permanently.** Roughly **75 of the 207 unclaimed lines repo-wide are false gaps.**

### False completeness — reported done, user cannot reach or use it

- **Chapters 9–12 at 100%.** The dominant instance: 46% of the book, extracted in full, with the mechanical half of every ability displayed as prose and applied by nothing. Nothing in the repo records this as a decision.
- **`ModifierResolver` — 0 app references.** CLAUDE.md §5 calls it "the highest-risk file"; it has extensive tests and no production caller. Documented as awaiting M2, and M4 shipped without it.
- **`DegreeOfSuccess` — 0 app references**, but reached transitively via `SkillRoll`/`Detection`/`Divination`. Not a real gap; flagged so the next sweep does not re-find it.
- **`SecondaryCharacteristics` — 0 app references**, reached via `Derive`. Same.

**A caveat that cut against task 1 — now settled.** This section originally suggested the right answer for chapters 9–12 might be a *documented refusal* on `Equipment.UNMODELLED`'s grounds — ownership, stacking, direction. **It is not.** All three fail here: `Character.beyonder` records the pathway and `PotionDigestion.digestThrough` folds the chain, so possession is derived rather than asserted; abilities are activated one at a time, so there is no stacking question; and a cost is unambiguously the actor's, so there is no direction question. It is unbuilt work.

That verdict is now recorded in `PotionDigestion.UNPARSED`, in production source where a sweep finds it, with three tests pinning it — including one asserting the record says "not a refusal", so a future reader cannot inherit it as settled.

**A design review then returned NO-SHIP on the obvious build**, and its findings are why task 1 is larger than "decompose the prose blob":

- **The price is not a number plus a boolean.** `SealedArtifacts`' `ArtifactCost.perTurn` is a `Boolean` rendered "per turn maintained". These abilities are priced in **15 distinct qualifiers across six kinds** — per turn *and* per minute (one turn is one second in this engine, so collapsing them is a 60× error), per-unit scaling ("per raven", "per 2 bullets"), action economy ("Free Action"), and one composite. A boolean discards five of six.
- **The obvious regex sees 173 of 237 printed SPI costs**, and **39 of 88 blocks** hold a cost it cannot see — priced in running prose ("Spend 1 SPI to activate"). A per-block list built from the named shape alone reads as exhaustive while dropping clauses: `an-invisible-line-reads-as-completion`, one layer down.
- **The entry point must take the chain, not the block.** Abilities are cumulative as stats and skills are; a `forBlock` API bypasses `digestThrough`'s three `require` guards.
- **`SealedArtifacts` governs the spend mechanism only, not possession** — `SealedArtifacts.all()` is ungated and offers every artifact to every character, so it never answered that question. My own decomposition cited it for possession and was wrong to.

My census in that decomposition was also wrong and a test now pins the real numbers: I wrote "one shape covers 165 of ~188", against a true denominator of **237**.
